# Mood colours

The palette uses familiar emotional cues, with labels and SF Symbols alongside colour. It does not
infer a person's feelings, diagnose a condition, or claim that a colour has one universal psychological meaning.

| Mood | Palette | Application |
|---|---|---|
| Happy | Warm sunshine yellow | Selected button, companion glow, mood labels/widget accent |
| Calm | Soft leaf green | Same |
| Sleepy | Twilight indigo | Same |
| A little low | Gentle blue | Same |
| Overwhelmed | Muted coral | Same |
| Focused | Golden amber | Same |

Yellow/joy and yellow-green/relaxation have experimental support in
[Jonauskaite et al., What color do you feel? (2019)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/col.22327).
Broader cross-country work finds both shared patterns and variation with language and geography:
[Jonauskaite et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797620948810).
The precise sleepy, overwhelmed and focused shades are design decisions, not scientifically established mappings.

The creature keeps its species colour; the mood controls its expression and surrounding UI. Monochrome/tinted
Lock Screen rendering is controlled by iOS, so names, mood words and symbols remain the primary information.
