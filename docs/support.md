# Dotlings Support

Little pets. Closer friends.

## Contact support

[Report a problem or ask a question](https://github.com/jxmils/dots/issues/new/choose). Support is provided by Jason Miller through GitHub. A GitHub account is needed to submit an issue; you can read this guide without one. Issues are public, so describe the problem without sharing private moods, Apple Account information or personal screenshots.

When reporting a problem, include your iPhone model, iOS version, Dotlings version, what you expected and what happened. There is no public support email address.

## Hatch and name a companion

Enter your first name or a nickname, rub the egg until it is warm, then give it three gentle taps. The Warm the shell button and accessibility actions offer an alternative to rubbing. Your new companion has a default name that you can change before taking it home. Names can also be changed later from Your profile.

## Choose your profile Dotling

Open Collection, choose an owned companion and tap Make this my Dotling. This becomes your home and profile companion and the one you share with invited friends. Hatching another companion keeps your current preference unless you explicitly switch.

## Add a friend

Open My circle and use the invitation button. Sign into iCloud in iPhone Settings to use friend sharing. An invitation lets someone see your current mood; ask them to send their own invitation if you want to see theirs. You each control your own share.

## Add a widget

Touch and hold your Lock Screen, choose Customize, select the Lock Screen and add Dotlings. Circular and rectangular widgets go below the clock; the inline widget goes in the text area above it. You can also use the Home Screen widget gallery. Choose the latest friend mood or pin a specific friend in the app or widget settings.

Widgets show the latest received mood and its original update time. iOS controls refresh timing. Open Dotlings and check for mood updates if a widget looks out of date. If access to a pinned friend is removed, choose another available friend.

## Collection and streaks

Your first random companion is free. Choose a mood on consecutive calendar days to earn more eggs at the milestones shown in Collection. One day counts once even if you update your mood several times. Reward eggs never duplicate a companion you own. A missed day can use one of three lifetime restores if any remain; otherwise a new streak begins. Collected companions and earned eggs remain yours after a streak resets. Restores do not refill after deleting a companion.

## Device storage and iCloud

Your collection and progress are stored per installation. Friend sharing uses iCloud, but the first version does not reconcile two devices independently changing the same owner's profile. Keep one active owner installation while using this version. Deleting the app may remove local progress, depending on your Apple backup and restore settings.

## Privacy and deletion

Open Your profile to manage sharing or delete your companion and shared mood. [Read the privacy policy](privacy.md) for storage, sharing and retention details, including the small lifetime-restore receipt.
