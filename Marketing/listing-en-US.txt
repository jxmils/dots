NAME

SUBTITLE
Little pets. Closer friends.

PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Your own squishy little companion. Hatch, name and play with your Dotling, share a mood with friends, and keep them close on your Lock Screen.

DESCRIPTION
Hatch a tiny companion, give them a name, and let them show how you're feeling.

Dotlings are squishy little pets with big expressions. Warm your first egg with a rub, give it three gentle taps, and see who's inside. Keep their name or choose your own, then bring them home.

How are you, really?
Choose happy, calm, sleepy, a little low, overwhelmed or focused. Your Dotling's expression changes with your mood. Tap them for a little reaction, or rub the screen to give them a gentle pet.

Keep your friends close
Invite a friend to see how you're feeling, and accept their invitation to see their mood too. Add a Lock Screen widget to keep one friend nearby, or see whoever last shared an update. There's a small Home Screen widget as well.

Meet the whole collection
There are 29 Dotlings to discover, including stars, plants, clouds, a coffee bean and a coconut. Your first companion is free. Check in each day to earn more eggs at streak milestones, starting at 5, 10, 25 and 50 days. Each reward brings someone new.

Miss a day? You have three lifetime streak restores. Even when a streak starts over, the companions and eggs you've already earned stay yours.

Pick your favorite
Choose any Dotling you've collected for your profile. Each keeps the name you've given them, so you can switch companions whenever you like.

You can hatch and play without creating an account. Sharing with friends requires iCloud, and you choose who gets to see your mood.

REVIEW NOTES
Dotlings is a virtual pet app with optional mood sharing between friends. There is no separate Dotlings account or app login. The profile contains a first name or nickname, a chosen companion and its name. It is not a registered user account.

To get started, enter a name, then rub the egg until it is warm. Alternatively, press Warm the shell four times. Tap the warmed shell three times to hatch. Keep or edit the companion's default name, then select Take them home.

Choose one of the six moods on the Companion screen. Tapping or rubbing the companion produces temporary play reactions and does not change the mood shared with friends.

Collection shows 29 companions. The first random companion is free. Daily mood check-ins earn new companions at the streak milestones shown in the app. There are three lifetime streak restores. Select an owned companion and tap Make this my Dotling to use it on the profile and in shared mood updates.

Friend sharing requires the device to be signed into iCloud. To test it, use two devices with different iCloud accounts. Send an invitation from My circle on the first device and accept it on the second. This lets the second person see the first person's current mood. Send an invitation back for sharing in both directions. Sharing can be managed from Your profile.

Widgets support circular, rectangular and inline Lock Screen layouts, plus a small Home Screen layout. Choose the latest friend mood or pin a friend. iOS controls refresh timing, so a widget may show the most recently received update rather than an immediate change.

Support and privacy links are in Your profile. The App Store screenshots show fictional sample profiles.
