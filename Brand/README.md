# Dotlings identity

Wordmark: lowercase dotlings, Nunito ExtraBold. Nunito is distributed under the SIL Open Font License (Fonts/OFL.txt). SVG wordmarks are outlined and need no installed font.

Palette:
- Ink: #332E42
- Warm cream: #FAF5ED
- Lilac: #E6D9F5
- Sky blue: #83C7FF

Use the dark wordmark on light backgrounds and the light wordmark on dark backgrounds. Leave at least the height of the lowercase o around the wordmark. Keep the mark and wordmark proportions intact. The friendly blue mascot is the app icon; do not add lettering to the icon.

Mascot.png and Hero.png are high-resolution marketing interpretations of the existing original creatures. They do not replace the 29 six-expression gameplay atlases. They were generated with the built-in image_gen tool using the exact prompts in prompts.json. Character identities, no-feet styling and the original collection remain intact.

The app's public name is Dotlings. Bundle IDs, App Group, CloudKit identifiers, storage keys, repository path and Xcode scheme keep their existing Dots values.
